Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information D2Cabs collects and how it is used when you book or use our service.

Information We Collect

When you book a ride, we collect details such as your name, phone number, email, pickup and drop locations, and payment information needed to complete your booking.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to confirm bookings, assign drivers, process payments, and contact you about your ride. We may also use it to improve our service and respond to support requests.

Sharing of Information

We share only the details necessary with the assigned driver to complete your trip. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please contact us if you notice anything suspicious about your account or booking.

Your Choices

You can contact us any time to ask what information we hold about you, or to request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to any bookings or legal records we are required to keep.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, reach out to us at d2cabs@gmail.com or +91 88896 33535.
