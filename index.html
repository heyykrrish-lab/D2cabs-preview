Frequently Asked Questions

What cab services does D2cabs offer?

D2cabs provides convenient cab services for local rides, airport transfers and outstation travel.

Do you provide local city rides?

Yes. You can request a cab for local travel within the city based on cab availability.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. Airport pickup and drop requests are available, including night transfers, subject to cab availability.

Can I book an outstation cab?

Yes. Share your route and travel schedule with D2cabs to check available outstation cab options.

Do you offer one-way cab service?

Yes. You can request a one-way trip when submitting your booking details.

Do you offer round-trip cab service?

Yes. Round-trip requests can be made by selecting the trip type that matches your travel requirement.

Can I choose my pickup location?

Yes. Enter the exact pickup point along with a useful landmark or location note in your booking.

Are airport transfers available 24/7?

Yes. Airport transfer requests are accepted 24/7, subject to cab availability.

Can I book a ride in advance?

Yes. Select your future date and time in the booking form and submit your request.

How do I confirm my booking?

Submit your booking request with the required trip details and wait for confirmation from D2cabs.

Can I modify my booking after confirmation?

Yes. Contact D2cabs support as early as possible and we will help you with available changes.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Contact D2cabs support as soon as possible to request cancellation and check the applicable terms.

What details are needed to book a cab?

You will need your pickup location, destination, travel date and time, and basic contact details.

Can I book a cab for someone else?

Yes. Provide the passenger's correct contact and pickup details while making the booking.

Can I book a one-way or round trip?

Yes. Choose the trip type that matches your travel requirement while submitting the booking request.

Can I request an outstation cab?

Yes. Share your route and travel schedule with D2cabs to check available outstation cab options.

Are drivers verified?

Yes. D2cabs works with verified, experienced drivers and shares cab details before departure.

Is airport pickup available at night?

Yes, airport transfer requests are accepted 24/7, subject to cab availability.

How will I receive my cab details?

Cab and driver details are shared before departure once your ride is confirmed.

Can I track my cab?

Ride tracking or driver updates depend on the booking and available service features.

Can I request a specific pickup point?

Yes. Enter the exact pickup point and any useful landmark or location note in your booking.

Can I carry luggage in the cab?

Yes. Normal travel luggage is allowed, subject to the available space in the selected cab.

What if my driver is delayed?

Contact D2cabs support if your driver is delayed so we can help with the ride status.

What should I do if I cannot find my cab?

Check the shared driver details and contact D2cabs support for assistance with the pickup.

What payment options are available?

You can complete payment securely using the options shown after your booking is accepted.

Do I need to pay in advance?

Payment requirements can vary by booking. Follow the payment instructions shown after your booking is accepted.

Can I pay in cash?

Cash availability depends on the ride and payment options offered for your confirmed booking.

Is online payment secure?

Yes. Use the secure payment option presented for your booking and avoid sharing payment credentials with anyone.

Will I receive a payment confirmation?

Payment confirmation is provided according to the payment method used for your booking.

What if I am charged the wrong amount?

Contact D2cabs support with your booking and payment details so the amount can be reviewed.

Are cancellation charges applicable?

Cancellation charges, if any, depend on the booking terms applicable to your ride.

How can I ask about a refund?

Contact D2cabs support with your booking details and we will guide you through the applicable refund process.
